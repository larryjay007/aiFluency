Lanre Odesanya Before transitioning into AI and machine learning, I worked in construction, where checking work against plans and specifications was part of my daily routine. That habit of verifying before approving has stayed with me. Today I apply the same mindset to machine learning: questioning a result before trusting it, rather than assuming a convincing output is necessarily a correct one. In practice, that means working as an ML evaluator for early-stage teams — checking a model's output against the evidence before it influences a real decision, not building the model myself. Currently in FlyRank's Applied Search Intelligence internship, building toward a decision-support prototype for content review prioritization. Lanre Odesanya — built plain, on purpose. No framework, no build step.
